Snapshot of Me

Hey there! I’m Sacha Hosana, a 17-year-old high schooler from Paris with big dreams of becoming an engineer and continuing my studies abroad.

A bit about my background: my mom is Czech-Slovak, my dad is Franco-Algerian, and I’ve got two brothers and two dogs. With that mix, I was lucky to grow up surrounded by diversity, open-mindedness, and a love for discovering new cultures and places. Traveling and exploring the world have been part of my life from the very beginning.

People often describe me as curious, and I’d say that’s pretty accurate. I love diving into new concepts and learning as much as I can. At the same time, I really value imagination, creativity, and communication, because I also enjoy creating and leading projects. I genuinely believe that with passion, curiosity, and hard-work, anything can become possible.
Some of the fields that fascinate me the most are Astronomy, Engineering, Science, and Economics. Outside of school, I’m learning German and Arabic, I do theatre, play guitar, read a lot, and take part in many exiting projects across various fields.
